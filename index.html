Hardened Security Architecture

Nine purpose-built layers engineered to survive real-world handset theft tactics.

1. Direct Boot Resumption

Runs in Device Encrypted Storage. If a thief reboots the device, SHE resumes tracking and SMS command listeners before the first user PIN entry.

2. Zero-Internet SMS Channel

Mobile data severed? Structured AT1 PDU frames authenticated by 48-bit truncated HMAC-SHA256 and monotonic counters trigger location replies over cellular SMS.

3. Zero-Knowledge Vault

Stores IMEIs, recovery seeds, and purchase receipts protected by client-side PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM. The server never holds the decryption keys.

4. Device Owner Kiosk Lock

Enforces hardware volume lockdown (DISALLOW_ADJUST_VOLUME), suppresses factory reset from settings, and pins the screen to the Emergency Return Card.

5. Intruder Photo Capture

Hooks into ACTION_SECURITY_PASSWORD_FAILED. Three consecutive wrong PIN attempts quietly trigger front-camera capture tagged with GPS coordinates.

6. 2FA & Single-Use Recovery

RFC 6238 TOTP with AES-256 encrypted secrets at rest, backed by 8 single-use Argon2id hashed emergency recovery codes designed for lost/stolen authenticator scenarios.

7. Mutual Camera QR Pairing

Zero cloud relay during handshake. Direct in-person camera QR exchange negotiates 256-bit symmetric pairing secrets verified with a mutual 3-digit human visual confirmation code.

8. Indian ERSS 112 & CEIR

One-touch emergency dialing for India National ERSS (112) and pre-formatted Sanchar Saathi CEIR data export (IMEI 1 & 2) for immediate nationwide telecom carrier blacklisting.

9. Zero-Telemetry Backend

High-performance FastAPI backend supporting SQLite and PostgreSQL, automated 30-day telemetry purging, zero external analytics trackers, and zero advertising SDKs.

Why Does Android & Google Play Protect Flag SHE Guardian as "High Risk"? Sideloading & OEM Installation Guide

When installing SHE Guardian on a secondary or friend's device, users frequently encounter an alarming full-screen banner from Google Play Protect or OEM package installers (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS, Oppo/Realme ColorOS, Vivo) declaring:

"Blocked by Play Protect" or "High Risk App Detected: This app can access your personal data, track your location, and manage your device."

For non-technical users, this warning sounds terrifying. But why does it happen, what is actually taking place under the hood, and how do you install SHE without being blocked?

1. The Architectural Reality: Why Heuristics Flag Anti-Theft Tools

Security scanners do not read source code; they use automated static heuristics to compare requested permissions against binary signatures. SHE Guardian triggers these heuristic alarms for two legitimate reasons:

  • 1. Open-Source Unsigned / Debug Keystore: Pre-release builds (such as app-guardian-direct-debug.apk) are compiled from source and signed with a self-generated development certificate, not an established Google Play developer account. Google's cloud reputation database has zero historical telemetry for this developer key.
  • 2. Deep Combat-Level Permissions: To fight theft in real life, SHE requires the exact same low-level capabilities that malicious spyware abuses:
    • BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN & Device Owner: Prevents the thief from uninstalling the app or lowering siren volume.
    • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: Tracks the stolen phone even while the screen is locked and no app is open.
    • SEND_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS: Executes offline AT1 cryptographic commands and broadcasts location beacons when cellular data/Wi-Fi is severed.
    • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Resumes Direct Boot tracking before the first lockscreen PIN is entered.
    • CAMERA: Silently photographs thieves after 3 consecutive wrong PIN attempts.
The Difference Between Malware and SHE: Malware hides these permissions and exfiltrates your data to commercial spyware brokers. SHE is 100% open-source under Apache 2.0, embeds zero third-party trackers or ad SDKs, performs zero cloud telemetry harvesting, and encrypts all recovery data with zero-knowledge client-side AES-256-GCM.

2. Step-by-Step Installation & Bypass Instructions by Device Brand

Follow the specific instructions below for your phone's operating system:

Google Play Protect (Pixel, Motorola, Nothing, Stock Android)
  1. When the "Blocked by Play Protect" modal appears, tap "More details" (or the small down arrow).
  2. Tap "Install anyway" (Unsafe).
  3. Alternative (If blocked completely): Open Google Play Store → Tap your Profile Icon (top right) → Tap Play Protect → Tap the Settings Gear (top right) → Turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect". Complete installation, then toggle it back on.
Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)
  1. When the package installer warns of "High Risk / Dangerous App" with a 10-second countdown timer:
  2. Wait patiently until the 10-second timer reaches 0.
  3. Check the circle: "I am aware of the possible risks and assume all possible consequences voluntarily."
  4. Tap "OK" or "Install" to proceed.
Samsung Galaxy (One UI / Knox)
  1. When the system displays "Unrecognized App / Blocked by Play Protect":
  2. Tap "More options" or "More details".
  3. Tap "Install anyway".
Oppo, Realme, Vivo & OnePlus (ColorOS, OxygenOS, OriginOS)
  1. If the Security Scan warns of an "Unknown Source / High Risk":
  2. Tap "Continue installing" or "Install anyway".
  3. Enter your device Lockscreen PIN / Fingerprint when prompted to authorize installation.

3. The Developer-Grade Method: ADB Install with Auto-Grant

If you have access to a computer with a USB cable, using ADB completely bypasses all interactive OEM installer friction dialogs and automatically approves all required runtime permissions in a single command:

adb install -r -g android/app-guardian/build/outputs/apk/direct/debug/app-guardian-direct-debug.apk

The -g flag instructs the Android Package Manager to pre-grant all requested permissions (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION, SEND_SMS, CAMERA, etc.) at installation time.

What If Your Only Phone Is Stolen? The Anti-Theft Guide to 2FA, Offline SMS, and Untrusted Acquaintances

Most two-factor authentication (2FA) systems share a dangerous structural flaw: they assume you still possess the phone that generates your security codes.

When a thief steals your primary device, you face the classic Authenticator Catch-22: You borrow a friend's phone to log into your tracking dashboard, but the login prompt demands a 6-digit TOTP code generated by Google Authenticator—an app sitting in the thief's pocket.

The SHE Defense Hierarchy: SHE resolves this dilemma through a multi-stage failover architecture designed specifically for users who carry only one phone.

Stage 1: Offline Emergency Recovery Codes (Paper Backup)

When you enroll Two-Factor Authentication, the SHE backend generates 8 single-use cryptographically hashed recovery codes (e.g., A3F9-8B2C). Write these down in a paper notebook at home or store them in a secure physical vault. When using an unfamiliar phone, tap "Use Emergency Recovery Code" to bypass the authenticator requirement instantly.

Stage 2: Cryptographic Offline SMS Remote Commands (Zero Internet Required)

If you are on the street and mobile data or Wi-Fi has been disabled by the thief, you do not need an active internet connection. Dispatched directly from the Admin Companion App via carrier SMS, SHE transmits structured AT1 Protocol Data Units (PDUs):

PDU Wire Format: CMD:<TYPE>|<SEQUENCE>|<EXPIRY>|<HMAC12>
• CMD:LOC • Requests an instant SMS beacon containing GPS coordinates and Google Maps link
• CMD:SIREN • Engages 100% volume alarm and looping voice alert (bypasses silent mode)
• CMD:LOCK • Enforces anti-theft kiosk screen takeover with emergency return contacts
Cryptographic SMS Verification: Plaintext SMS commands like #WIPE or #LOC 1234 do not exist in SHE. Every inbound SMS command is authenticated with a 48-bit truncated HMAC-SHA256 derived from the 256-bit symmetric shared secret established during camera QR pairing. A strictly monotonic sequence counter (sequenceNumber > lastSeq) blocks SMS replay attacks, and an epoch timestamp ensures expired commands are dropped. Destructive commands like CLEAR_DEVICE_OWNER are strictly prohibited over SMS and require an authenticated online session with fresh 2FA TOTP.

Stage 3: What If An Acquaintance or "Friend" Stole It?

Internal handset theft by someone you know poses unique challenges: they may have glanced at your lockscreen PIN or know your daily habits. SHE addresses this with strict boundary isolation:

1. Zero-Knowledge Master Passphrase: The SHE Vault uses a separate master passphrase (≥ 16 characters) that is never saved on disk. Knowing your lockscreen PIN does not grant access to your stored banking recovery codes or documents.
2. Hardware Volume Lock: Under Stolen Mode, the physical volume-down button is locked at the Android kernel level (DISALLOW_ADJUST_VOLUME), preventing the thief from muffling the siren.
3. Kiosk Return Lock: Remote lock pins the screen to the Return Screen, preventing them from accessing WhatsApp, Telegram, or Google Photos.

SMS Permissions, Banking OTPs & Malicious Links: How SHE Enforces Zero-Retention Privacy & Sandboxed Defense

When an anti-theft app requests SEND_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions, security-conscious users rightly ask:

"If this app falls into the wrong hands, could it steal my banking OTPs, send unauthorized spam texts, or open malicious links?"

The short answer is absolutely not. SHE is designed with cryptographic boundaries and architectural isolation that make OTP interception, spamming, and link manipulation technically impossible. Here is the exact technical proof:

1. Why It Is Impossible for SHE to Steal Banking OTPs or Private Texts

  • Zero Inbox Access (No READ_SMS): Android strictly separates incoming broadcast listeners from reading existing text messages. SHE Guardian does not request android.permission.READ_SMS. It has zero ability to query your SMS inbox, inspect past messages, or access stored banking history.
  • Immediate Zero-Retention Discard: The moment Android dispatches an incoming SMS broadcast, Guardian evaluates the message on the very first line of code in SmsCommandReceiver.kt:
    if (!body.trim().startsWith("AT1:CMD:")) { continue }
    Any SMS that does not begin with the strict AT1:CMD: cryptographic frame (such as a 6-digit bank OTP, Aadhaar verification, or personal chat) is instantly discarded from memory in 0 microseconds. It is never logged, never saved to disk, and never transmitted over the internet.
  • Standard OS Delivery: SHE does not consume or abort SMS broadcasts. Your default messaging app (Google Messages, Samsung Messages) receives your OTP completely unobstructed.

2. Why It Is Impossible to Send Spam or Malicious Links

A malicious SMS app typically sends spam to premium-rate numbers or contacts. SHE prevents this at the architecture level:

  • Strictly Pinned Trusted Recipient: Guardian cannot send an SMS to arbitrary numbers. Outgoing SMS is hardcoded to route strictly to storage.getTrustedPhoneNumber()—the owner's Admin Companion number verified during mutual camera QR pairing. If no trusted number is configured, outgoing SMS is dropped entirely.
  • Rigid Non-Customizable Templates: Outgoing SMS messages cannot be scripted or manipulated. The only SMS messages Guardian can ever construct are:
    • Cryptographic AT1 location response: AT1:RPT:LOC|<alias>|<lat>|<lng>|...
    • Theft alert with device GPS fix: [AntiTheft ALERT] Handset '...' marked STOLEN. GPS: https://maps.google.com/?q=...
    No command or attacker can alter this format or inject phishing URLs.

3. Why It Is Impossible to Open Malicious Links

Can a remote command force Guardian to open a phishing link or malware download? No, because Guardian contains:

  • 0 WebViews: Guardian has zero embedded web browsing engines.
  • 0 ACTION_VIEW Browser Intents: There is zero code that invokes an external web browser.
  • 0 Scripting Engines: Remote commands are strictly constrained to an internal hardware enum: LOCATE (fetches GPS chip coordinates), RING (plays local siren audio), STOP_RING (mutes siren), and LOCK / STOLEN_MODE (pins screen via Android DevicePolicyManager).

4. SMS Permissions Are 100% Optional

If you prefer not to grant SMS permissions to SHE Guardian on your device:

Complete Freedom to Deny SMS: You can simply revoke or deny SMS permission in Settings → Apps → SHE Guardian → Permissions → SMS → "Don't allow".
The app will continue to function 100% normally! Live GPS tracking, siren drills, remote kiosk lock, intruder front-camera capture, and zero-knowledge vault backups will operate seamlessly over Wi-Fi and mobile data via the encrypted FastAPI backend. SMS is solely an emergency offline fallback when mobile data has been severed.

High-Precision GPS Telemetry, Direct Boot Tracking & One-Click Map Search: How SHE Pinpoints Stolen Devices

When a smartphone is stolen, every minute matters. Tracking must be immediate, precise, and verifiable. SHE combines hardware GPS satellite fixes, Android Direct Boot cryptographic storage, and one-touch map navigation to pinpoint handsets while strictly preserving zero-knowledge privacy.

1. Real-Time Hardware GPS & Fused Triangulation

SHE Guardian queries Android's native location providers (combining GPS satellites, GLONASS, Galileo, and cell-tower trilateration). Rather than sending raw or unverified strings:

  • Hardware Cryptographic Signatures: Every single coordinate packet (latitude, longitude, altitude, velocity, and accuracy) is digitally signed with the handset's hardware-backed ECDSA P-256 private key stored in the device Keystore. Coordinates cannot be spoofed by attackers or man-in-the-middle proxies.
  • High-Precision Coordinates: Location fixes are encoded to 5 decimal places (approx. 1.1-meter physical resolution).

2. Direct Boot Resumption: Tracking Before First Unlock

A standard anti-theft app stops working if the thief simply powers off the phone or forces a reboot. Why? Because Credential Encrypted Storage (CE) remains locked until the user enters their lockscreen PIN.

The Direct Boot Advantage: SHE Guardian operates entirely within Device Encrypted Storage (DES). As soon as the Linux kernel boots, Guardian's tracking foreground service and SMS receivers initialize immediately. If a thief turns on the phone and leaves it locked, location telemetry is already streaming to your private server.

3. One-Click "Search on Map" Integration

In an emergency, you do not have time to manually copy, paste, or type coordinate numbers into a search engine. Both the SHE Admin Companion App and the Web Dashboard feature a dedicated "Search on Google Maps" action:

  • Native Android Intent: In the Admin Companion App, tapping Search on Map executes Intent.ACTION_VIEW with standard geo:lat,lng?q=lat,lng URI, dropping an immediate red pin on Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.
  • Web Portal Search: On the web dashboard, clicking Search on Google Maps opens a direct satellite/street view navigation pin in a secure new tab.

4. Understanding Accuracy Margins & Timestamps

  • Accuracy Margin (±meters): Represents the 68% confidence radius. Values under ±15m indicate direct satellite lock; larger values (±500m) indicate cellular cell-tower fallback.
  • Capture Time vs. Server Receipt: Capture time shows the exact microsecond the GPS chip calculated the fix; Server Receipt shows when your private backend verified the hardware ECDSA signature.
100% Zero-Telemetry Guarantee: Your coordinates are never sent to third-party ad brokers, analytics SDKs, or cloud aggregators. Telemetry is saved strictly in your private database and subjected to an automated 30-day rolling purge.

How Handset Location Tracking Works

1. Real-Time GPS & Fused Satellites:
Guardian queries Android's hardware GPS and network location providers at regular intervals, or on-demand when the owner triggers a LOCATE command. Each fix includes latitude, longitude, altitude, and precision radius.

2. Tamper-Proof Cryptographic Signatures:
Before leaving the phone, each coordinate packet is cryptographically signed using the handset's hardware-backed ECDSA P-256 private key stored in Android Keystore. The backend verifies this signature before storing the fix, preventing GPS spoofing attacks.

3. Direct Boot Resumption (Pre-PIN Tracking):
Even if a thief power-cycles the phone and leaves it at the lockscreen, Guardian starts automatically in Device Encrypted Storage (DES) and continues broadcasting location updates without needing the user's PIN to be entered.

4. Margin of Accuracy (±meters):
The accuracy number represents the 68% confidence radius in meters. Values under ±20m represent high-precision satellite triangulation; larger values represent cellular tower approximations.

5. 100% Zero-Telemetry Guarantee:
Coordinates are stored exclusively in your own private, self-hosted database. SHE contains zero third-party trackers, zero Google Analytics, and enforces an automated 30-day rolling purge.
