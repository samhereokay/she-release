Anti-Theft & Handset Recovery Ecosystem: User Guide

A comprehensive operational runbook for handset owners. Explains everyday management, emergency response during theft, encrypted vault storage, and official Indian CEIR reporting.

1. How the System Works: The Two Apps

The ecosystem consists of two distinct Android applications communicating securely:

Capability Guardian App (Protected Phone) Admin App (Controller Phone)
Primary Purpose Silent background tracking & combat defense Remote management, live map & recovery vault
Background Services Direct Boot foreground tracking & SMS listener On-demand polling & command dispatcher
Hardware Controls Device Owner volume lock, kiosk screen takeover Hardware Keystore ECDSA private key signing
Recovery Vault Does not store vault secrets (prevents theft leak) Zero-knowledge AES-256-GCM vault decryptor

2. Sideloading: Bypassing Google Play Protect & OEM "High Risk" Warnings

Why Android Flags SHE Guardian:
When sideloading pre-release builds onto a secondary handset, Google Play Protect and OEM package installers (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS/MIUI, ColorOS, OriginOS) will flag Guardian as "High Risk" or "Blocked by Play Protect". This is an automated heuristic response triggered because Guardian combines Device Admin, Background Location, and SMS execution under a local open-source developer certificate.

How to Install Without Errors:
  • Google Play Protect (Stock / Motorola / Pixel): Tap "More details" (down arrow) → Tap "Install anyway". If completely blocked, open Play Store → Profile → Play Protect → Settings gear → temporarily toggle off "Scan apps with Play Protect".
  • Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (HyperOS / MIUI): Wait for the 10-second security countdown → Check "I am aware of the possible risks" → Tap "OK" / "Install".
  • Samsung Galaxy (One UI): Tap "More options" → Tap "Install anyway".
  • Oppo / Realme / Vivo: Tap "Continue installing" → Enter your lockscreen PIN/fingerprint when prompted.
  • Cleanest Method (ADB): Connect phone to PC and execute: adb install -r -g app-guardian-direct-debug.apk (the -g flag auto-grants permissions and bypasses all UI warnings).
SMS Channel Privacy & Zero-Retention OTP Protection:
• No Inbox Access: Guardian does not request READ_SMS and cannot access stored banking history or existing messages.
• Immediate OTP Discard: Any incoming text that does not start with AT1:CMD: (bank OTPs, personal SMS) is discarded in 0 microseconds without logging or disk storage.
• Strictly Pinned Recipient: Outgoing SMS can only ever reach your registered Admin Companion phone number.
• Zero Link Opening: Guardian contains 0 WebViews and 0 URL launching code; it cannot open web links.
• 100% Optional: SMS permissions can be revoked anytime. The app will continue to track, sound sirens, and lock via Wi-Fi and mobile data.

3. Day-to-Day Operation

Checking Live Location

Open the Admin Companion App dashboard. Tap your protected phone's alias to view its latest location fix, battery level, accuracy radius, and last-seen timestamp.

Testing Alarms Safely: "Test Mode" vs. "Real Stolen Mode"

When verifying that speakers and tracking work, never activate real stolen mode in casual settings:

  • TEST_MODE (Drill): Plays a safe, non-destructive 10-second siren and voice alert. It automatically shuts off after 10 seconds, does not lock your device, and does not send emergency SMS alerts to your family contacts.
  • STOLEN_MODE (Real Combat): Full combat lockdown. Loops siren and voice alert continuously at maximum volume, locks the physical volume-down button, takes over the screen with the Emergency Return Card, and dispatches automated emergency SMS messages with Google Maps links.

Using the Encrypted Recovery Vault

The Recovery Vault is your digital safety deposit box. It uses client-side AES-256-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations). Even if the backend server database is compromised, your secrets cannot be read.

  • Device Identifiers: Store IMEI 1, IMEI 2, and Hardware Serial Number for police reports.
  • Account Recovery Codes: Google, banking, and cloud backup 2FA recovery seeds.
  • Emergency Contacts: Family phone numbers and critical email addresses.

4. What to Do If Your Phone Is Stolen (4-Step Workflow)

  1. Step 1: Activate Stolen Mode
    If the handset has internet, engage STOLEN_MODE from the Admin Companion App. If the thief shut off mobile data or Wi-Fi, the Admin App automatically sends an authenticated AT1 SMS command directly to the handset.
  2. Step 2: What the Thief Experiences
    The phone immediately starts blaring a siren at 100% volume accompanied by looping Android Text-To-Speech: "Warning. This device is reported stolen. Location is being tracked and reported. Return to owner immediately." Physical volume buttons cannot silence it, and the screen is locked to the Emergency Return Card.
  3. Step 3: Indian ERSS 112 & Sanchar Saathi CEIR Blacklisting
    Filing a police report and carrier blacklisting are two sequential actions:
    • Action A (Police Complaint / FIR): Dial 112 (India National ERSS) for active pursuit, or file an e-FIR on your State Police portal. Provide live GPS coordinates and IMEIs retrieved from your Admin Vault. Obtain the stamped FIR / Complaint Reference.
    • Action B (CEIR Blacklist): Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in (CEIR portal). Submit a "Block Stolen/Lost Mobile" request with your FIR copy and IMEI numbers to permanently blacklist the device across all Indian telecom networks (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL).
  4. Step 4: De-escalation Upon Recovery
    Once recovered, open the Admin Companion App, tap the device, and dispatch EXIT_KIOSK to restore normal operation.

5. Honest Technical Boundaries: What No App Can Prevent

Security software must set honest expectations. No application can bypass physical laws:

  • Hardware Force-Off / Battery Pull: A thief who holds the physical power button for 10–15 seconds triggers a motherboard-level cut-off. Software cannot run while powered off.
  • Faraday Bags / Signal Blockers: Radio-frequency shielding bags block GPS and cellular signals. Guardian logs fixes internally and burst-syncs the moment signal returns.
  • Professional Chip-Off Exploitation: Advanced syndicates with EEPROM programmers can overwrite flash memory chipsets directly. Hardware FRP and Device Owner restrictions make this economically non-viable for opportunistic thieves, but cannot prevent hardware destruction.
